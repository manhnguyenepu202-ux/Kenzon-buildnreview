The Bambu Lab P1S is a closed, fast CoreXY printer that works well out of the box. Most owners still add a few extras within the first month: something to handle fumes, a way to print in several colours, decent filament and a spare build plate. Below are four accessories that come up again and again in Amazon listings and owner reviews. We haven't tested any of them hands-on. Everything here comes from the product listings and the customer reviews published there, so treat it as research, not a lab result.

## 1. Mintion Filter System (HEPA H13 + Activated Carbon)

Printing ABS, ASA or nylon in a room you live in is where most people start thinking about air. The Mintion V1 is a small external unit with a pre-filter, a HEPA H13 layer and an activated carbon bed, driven by a fan with an adjustable speed. The listing says it ships with a connector for the P1S and X1C, and it can be adapted to other enclosed printers with printed adapters.

**What reviewers say:** several owners report the smell of ABS or ASA disappearing, and tool-free filter swaps get praise. The most common complaint is noise at higher fan speeds. One reviewer also broke a printed mount while peeling off the tape used to ship it assembled.

**Best for:** anyone printing ABS, ASA, PETG or nylon indoors without a window to vent through. If you only print PLA, it is a nice extra, not a must.

## 2. Bambu Lab AMS (Automatic Material System)

The AMS holds four spools, loads filament automatically, switches to a backup spool when one runs out and reads RFID tags on Bambu filament. It is the route to multicolour printing on a P1S, and the listing says up to four units can be linked for 16 colours.

**What reviewers say:** the listing's rating is only moderate, and the written reviews are split. Some owners say it works well. Others report feeding errors, filament it won't retract and tag-recognition problems. A few of these may be unlucky units, so check the return terms. The listing's own title mentions the A1 series while its text talks about the P1P, so confirm compatibility with your exact printer on the listing before buying. If you are choosing a new printer anyway, a Combo that includes the AMS may be simpler.

**Best for:** people who want multicolour or automatic spool switching and accept that multicolour printing wastes some filament.

## 3. Bambu Lab PLA Matte (1 kg, RFID, with Spool)

Matte PLA has a soft, non-reflective finish that hides layer lines better than glossy PLA. Bambu's version carries an RFID tag, so an AMS identifies the filament and applies its settings on its own. It comes in many colours, and the spool is reusable.

**What reviewers say:** the rating is high and owners like the finish and the consistent feeding. A small number report tight winding or clogs, with one reviewer warning specifically about the Charcoal colour, so inspect the first metres of any new spool.

**Best for:** display pieces, enclosures and anything where you want a refined look. Keep it for the AMS if you want the RFID benefit.

## 4. Giorbo Textured PEI Build Plate

A second plate lets you swap one while the other cools, and a textured PEI plate gives prints a grippy first layer and a matte underside. The Giorbo plate is double-sided, made of spring steel, and carries a QR code for printers that identify the plate. The listing names the P1S, P2S, A1, X1 and X2D.

**What reviewers say:** owners describe it as fitting like the stock plate with good adhesion and easy release. Note that many of the reviews on the listing are marked as Vine reviews of free product, and the listing has only a few dozen ratings. One reviewer mentions fingerprints reducing adhesion until the plate was wiped with isopropyl alcohol.

**Best for:** anyone who wants a low-cost spare plate or runs back-to-back jobs.

## Which One First?

If you print enclosed materials indoors, start with the filter. If colour is the goal, look at the AMS, or at a printer that includes one. Filament and a spare plate are cheap and useful for everyone. Prices and stock change often, so check the current price on Amazon before you order.
