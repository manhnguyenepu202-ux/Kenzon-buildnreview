## Design

The L-shaped frame has a small footprint and fits on a desk. The hotend comes off with one hand and nozzle swaps need no tools.

## Experience

Auto levelling and vibration compensation mean the very first print looks good. It is quiet enough to run overnight.

## Should you buy it?

If you have never owned a 3D printer, this is the most sensible place to start.
