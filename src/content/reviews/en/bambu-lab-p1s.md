## Setup

From box to first print takes about 20 minutes. The printer levels its bed, measures resonance and calibrates flow before each print.

## Print quality

With PLA on the standard profile, surfaces are even with almost no ringing. The enclosure holds heat well, so large ABS parts warp less.

## Should you buy it?

If you want a machine that simply works every day, the P1S is hard to fault. If you only print small PLA parts, the A1 mini costs far less.
