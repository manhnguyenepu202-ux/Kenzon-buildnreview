## Handling

At about 1 kg it sits comfortably in the hand and fits inside cabinets.

## Performance

It drives 5 × 50 mm wood screws easily, but struggles with large bits in hardwood.

## Should you buy it?

Yes, if you want one drill for the house and value low weight.
