## Reliability

A load cell in the print head probes the bed accurately everywhere, so first layers are almost always right.

## Maintenance

Every part has a detailed replacement guide. That is a real difference from budget printers.

## Should you buy it?

If the printer earns you money and needs to keep running, the MK4S is worth the price.
